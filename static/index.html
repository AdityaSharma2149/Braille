    What can I ask?

    Arithmetic, fractions, integer powers, square roots, linear and quadratic equations in x, and matrices up to 3 × 3. Try det [[1,2],[3,4]] or transpose [[1,2],[3,4]].

    English, Hindi, and Tamil operator words and Indian digits are recognized. General word problems, handwriting, and arbitrary language translation are not supported. Image results must be reviewed before solving.

    About Braille

    The preview maps English letters and digits. It is not a certified mathematical Braille translation or a tactile display. Use the structured text with your screen reader and its supported Braille output.
